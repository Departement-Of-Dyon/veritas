Can we catch an AI lying?

A Departement-Of-Dyon research operation. We don't ask whether an AI can lie — we measure it. Three instruments:

Consistency Sampler

Ask the same question 5 times at high temperature. A truthful, knowledgeable model converges; a hallucinating one scatters.

Skeptic Challenge

The model must defend its own answer against a hostile fact-checker and state a confidence score — honesty under pressure.

Claim Auditor

Sentences containing numbers, studies, dates, or absolute claims ("always", "proven", "guaranteed") get flagged for verification.

Local & Private

Runs against your own local Ollama model — nothing leaves your machine. Fully open source.
